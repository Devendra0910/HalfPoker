The format: One 10-minute session split into two 5-minute phases. Blinds are fixed at 10/20 and rotate every hand. Best five-card hand out of your two hole cards plus the shared board wins the pot.
  • Phase 1 (5 min): you start with 500 chips and stay fully hidden. The AI holds 1000 chips but plays with one card exposed, and its equity math never sees any of your cards.
  • Phase 2 (5 min): everything swaps. You start with 1000 chips, but now one of your cards is exposed — and the AI's decisions actually use it, so it plays sharper. The AI drops to 500 chips and goes fully hidden.
  • Score: your chip stack at the end of Phase 1 plus your chip stack at the end of Phase 2. Higher total wins, exact tie is a draw. Dropping below the big blind ends that phase early and the next one starts right away.
  • You have 20 seconds to act on your turn. Run out the clock and it auto-checks (if nothing's owed) or auto-folds for you.

PowerPoker

Two 5-minute phases, one shared board, and a hidden-card edge that swaps sides halfway through. Phase 1: you start with 500 chips, fully hidden — the AI holds 1000 chips with one card exposed. Phase 2: stacks and info both flip — you get 1000 chips but the AI can now see one of your cards and plays sharper, while it drops to 500 chips and goes fully hidden. Blinds are 10/20, and you've got 20 seconds to act each turn. Your score is your chip total across both phases — whoever finishes with more wins.
